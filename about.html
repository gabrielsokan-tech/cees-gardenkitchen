About Us

Our story, our mission and our promise.

Who we are

Cees Garden Kitchen is a Nigerian restaurant located at Cees Garden Estate in Abuja. We prepare and serve traditional Nigerian meals, snacks and grills for dine-in, takeaway, delivery and events.

Our story

The restaurant started as a small home kitchen serving neighbours in the estate. Word spread about the food, and today we serve guests from all over Abuja while keeping the same warm, family-style cooking.

Our mission

To serve delicious, affordable and hygienically prepared Nigerian food with excellent customer service.

Our vision

To become Abuja's most loved home of authentic Nigerian cuisine.

Our objectives

  • Maintain consistent food quality
  • Support local farmers and suppliers
  • Keep a clean, welcoming space
  • Deliver on time, every time
